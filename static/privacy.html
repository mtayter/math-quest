🧮 Math Quest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Math Quest is a small math-practice website run as a personal project. This policy explains what data it collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and profile picture, as provided by Google. These identify your account and personalize the site.
  • When you play: your answers to practice problems (correct/incorrect, number of attempts, hints used), XP, levels, badges, streaks, and per-topic progress. This is your own practice history.
  • Session cookie: a single cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. It is HttpOnly and is not used for tracking or advertising.

What we do with it

  • Your practice history powers the site itself: adaptive difficulty, badges, and your own progress display.
  • Leaderboard: your name, XP, level, streak, and per-topic accuracy are visible to other signed-in players on the Leaderboard. Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. There are no ads and no analytics trackers.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, or anything outside what is listed above. Google handles the sign-in itself; Math Quest never sees your Google password.

Your choices

  • Sign out any time from the home screen.
  • Delete your data: contact the site operator to have your account and practice history deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change.
